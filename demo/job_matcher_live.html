Your candidate profile

Matching needs skills, experience, and education, exactly like the real system. Edit your skills above and run it again to see the ranking change.

This is the live system. Every result below is returned by your running backend: this page saves your profile with PUT /api/v1/candidates/<id>/profile, reads jobs from the database with GET /api/v1/jobs/, and runs the match with POST /api/v1/matches. The scores and ranking are computed by the server, not by this page.
Known limitation: skills are matched by exact word (case-insensitive), so "JS" will not match "JavaScript". A skill-synonym layer is planned.
